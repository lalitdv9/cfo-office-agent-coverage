## Abstract (condensed, from fetched text)
Develops an integrative framework for "agentic finance": market environments in which autonomous/semi-autonomous AI systems participate in information processing, decision support, and execution. Three steps: (1) a four-layer architecture of financial AI agents (data perception, reasoning engine, strategy generation, execution & control); (2) the Agentic Financial Market Model (AFMM) linking agent design parameters (autonomy depth, model heterogeneity, execution coupling, infrastructure concentration, supervisory observability) to market outcomes (efficiency, liquidity resilience, volatility, systemic risk); (3) an empirical research agenda using public financial/textual data. Central argument: systemic implications depend less on model intelligence than on how agent architectures are distributed, coupled, and governed; near-term equilibrium is "bounded autonomy" (supervised co-pilots, monitoring systems, constrained execution modules).
