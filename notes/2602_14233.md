## Why cited (§2 + §6)
Direct evidence that even the evaluation-VALIDITY reform literature for financial LLMs
does not reach the CFO office or control dimensions:
- Five biases ("five sins"), definitions verbatim (Intro): look-ahead ("information that
  was not available at decision time"), survivorship ("evaluation universe silently drops
  firms that merge, delist, or fail"), narrative ("clean rationale that is not supported
  by the evidence available at the time"), objective ("training and alignment reward
  confident completion instead of calibrated uncertainty and safe refusal"), cost
  ("gross performance while assuming zero execution friction and zero operating cost").
  Framing register: backtests, delisted firms, trading frictions (markets); narrative and
  objective are general model-behavior biases.
- **Zero-hit verification (grep over full text):** "CFO", "controllership",
  "reconciliat*", "corporate finance", "internal control" — no matches anywhere.
- Abstract: "structural validity should be enforced before any result is used to support
  a deployment claim" — the market-side analogue of our §6 capability-vs-deployability
  gate; cited as such in §6.
- Abstract: "We reviewed 164 papers from 2023 to 2025 and found that no single bias is
  discussed in more than 28 percent of studies." (Intro adds: look-ahead acknowledged in
  26.8%, survivorship 1.2%.)
- Proposes a Structural Validity Framework + evaluation checklist (their Appendix C
  "Template For Checklist") — precedent for our Appendix C checklist form.
