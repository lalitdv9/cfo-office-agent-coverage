# TheAgentCompany: Benchmarking LLM Agents on Consequential Real World Tasks

- **ID:** arXiv:2412.14161 (v3, 10 Sep 2025; v1 18 Dec 2024) — verified from https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14161 + https://arxiv.org/html/2412.14161v3 (full text)
- **Authors:** Frank F. Xu, Yufan Song, Boxuan Li, ... Graham Neubig (CMU et al.)
- **Venue:** arXiv preprint (comments: "Preprint")
- **Status:** INCLUDE — category: cross-domain (E6 rule: general digital-worker benchmark with an expert-curated Finance task subset; analysis restricted to Finance (+ finance-flavored Admin) tasks)

## Summary
Self-contained simulated software-company environment (self-hosted GitLab, ownCloud, Plane, Rocket.Chat) where agents browse the web, write code, run programs, and message LLM-simulated coworkers (NPCs). 175 expert-curated tasks across departments: 69 SDE, 28 PM, 14 DS, 15 Admin, 29 HR, 12 Finance, rest Other; tasks referenced against the O*NET database of job responsibilities. Checkpoint-based graded evaluation with partial credit.

## Finance-function subset (basis for cross-domain include)
- **12 Finance tasks** (Appendix B): "For the 15 administrative, 29 human resources and 12 finance tasks, we collaborated with two senior HR/admin staff professionals. Both have extensive professional experience in HR, finance, and administrative operations."
- Worked Finance example (Table 3): "Navigate to ownCloud ... and complete Section B—Alternative Simplified Credit of IRS Form 6765" gathering data from /Documents/Financials/TAC_financials.csv and research_wages.csv, consulting instructions PDF, and contacting "the finance director (David Wong) on Rocket.Chat" for ambiguous questions — i.e., R&D-credit tax-form preparation from company financial data with human-in-loop clarification.
- Sec 7.2: "some Admin and Finance tasks involve making spreadsheets, collecting and filling in a lot of information from various people, or understanding images scanned by employees."

## Leaf cells
- **4.2 ◐ (P)** — Compliance & filings: preparation of an IRS Form 6765 (Alternative Simplified Credit) section from financial/wage data; single form section in a simulated company, not multi-jurisdiction filing.
- **2.5 ◐ (P)** — Financial modeling & spreadsheet operations: finance/admin tasks "involve making spreadsheets" and consolidating information from coworkers; generic workbook construction, not driver-based modeling.

## Eval method
Checkpoint-based graded scoring: each task decomposed into weighted checkpoints; deterministic programmatic checks plus LLM-based evaluator for artifact quality ("the report does not meet quality standards as assessed by the LLM-based evaluator"); partial-completion score formula rewarding incremental progress; also efficiency (token-cost) metric.

## Data realism
Fully simulated but high-fidelity: synthetic company data (TAC_financials.csv etc.) in a reproducible self-hosted environment; LLM NPC coworkers with "realistic traps and pitfalls"; tasks hand-curated by domain experts (incl. two senior HR/finance/admin professionals) from O*NET job responsibilities. No real corporate data.

## Agent scope
Single baseline agent (OpenHands CodeAct-style) with browser + bash terminal + Python + chat-platform interfaces; long-horizon multi-application workflows requiring NPC interaction; tested over closed and open-weights LLMs.

## Key numbers (quoted)
- Abstract: "the most competitive agent can complete 30% of tasks autonomously."
- Sec 7.2: "DS, Admin, and Finance tasks are the lowest, with many LLMs completing none of the tasks successfully."
- Sec 7.2: "LLMs fail these seemingly easier tasks due to lack of ability to understand documents, communicate with other people, navigate complex software and tedious processes"; "administrative and financial tasks, are usually private data within companies, not readily available for training LLMs."

## Survey use
Cross-domain anchor showing finance/admin back-office tasks are among the HARDEST categories for current agents (inversion vs SWE); supports RQ on data-scarcity of private finance workflows; checkpoint+NPC evaluation design relevant to close-orchestration/eval-methodology discussion. Restrict claims to the 12-task Finance subset (+ finance-flavored Admin tasks).
