# IPO Finance Agent: Evaluation of LLM Financial Analysts beyond Finance Agent v2, with Automated Rubric Generation — the Case of the SpaceX (SPCX) IPO

- arXiv ID: 2606.23032
- Authors: Mostapha Benhenda (single author, gmail affiliation)
- Status: full-text fetched: Y (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.23032v1)

## Summary
Extends the Vals AI Finance Agent v2 (FABv2) investor-research benchmark from periodic filings (10-K/10-Q) to IPO due diligence over S-1 registration statements, using the SpaceX (SPCX) S-1 as flagship case. Two contributions beyond FABv2: (a) task domain — 1,000 IPO-diligence questions (70 on the SpaceX S-1 released publicly; 930 private against contamination); (b) retrieval architecture — the original FABv2 harness failed to complete on the long S-1, so the author substitutes contextual retrieval (Anthropic-style chunk enrichment). Also introduces an evaluator-optimizer pipeline that auto-generates grading rubrics from an ensemble of independently generated model answers.

## Tasks covered (task design)
Six IPO-diligence domains spanning the analytical surface of an S-1: segment economics & operating performance; KPI quality & monetization; governance & control structures; accounting & common-control/pro-forma treatments; plus capital-formation and risk-disclosure domains. Questions additionally carry audience labels (equity analyst, credit analyst, securities counsel, accounting/transaction advisory) enabling result slicing by audience.

## Eval method
Rubric-based grading with automatically generated rubrics (candidate facts extracted from ensembles of model answers, then filtered), explicitly aiming to cut manual annotation effort; cost-per-query tracked alongside accuracy.

## Data realism
Real SEC S-1 filing (SpaceX/SPCX, treated as filed; ref [33]); questions author-constructed. Caveats: single-company public split (benchmark risks overfitting to one company's idiosyncrasies — acknowledged), auto-generated rubrics not fully human-validated, no controlled ablation isolating retrieval-vs-model contributions, no multi-run variance (flagged as future work).

## Agent scope
Agentic harness (retrieval-augmented analyst agent over a long filing); single-agent QA loop, not multi-agent.

## Key numbers
- IPO Finance Agent leaderboard: Qwen 3.7 Max 79.4% at $0.30/query; MiMo 2.5 Pro 76.8% at $0.05/query.
- Comparison: Finance Agent v2 ceiling Gemini 3.5 Flash 57.9% at $2.51/query; Claude Fable 5 56.3% at $8.06; FABv2 cheapest competitive entry MiniMax M3 48.3% at $0.32.
- 1,000 questions total; 70 public (SpaceX S-1), remainder private.

## Relevance to CFO-office taxonomy
- ADJ-1 analyst/financial-research band (investor/deal-side diligence over public filings), per the standing Checkpoint-1 ruling — same band as Finance Agent Benchmark (2508.00828) which it extends.
- NOT scored as core 6.2 due diligence: the tasks are analyst-side S-1 reading, not company-side M&A/finance-office diligence.
- Accounting/common-control question domain touches technical-accounting comprehension but from the reader's side.
