# Better Bill GPT: Comparing Large Language Models against Legal Invoice Reviewers

- arXiv ID: 2504.02881
- Authors: Nick Whitehouse, Nicole Lincoln, Stephanie Yiu, Lizzie Catterson, Rivindu Perera (AI Center of Excellence, Onit Inc, New Zealand)
- Status: full-text fetched: Y (https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02881v1)

## Summary
First empirical head-to-head comparison of LLMs against human legal-invoice reviewers (Early-Career Lawyers, Experienced Lawyers, Legal Operations Professionals) on billing-compliance review: deciding whether invoices and individual line items should be approved or disputed under contractual billing guidelines. LLMs beat all human groups on accuracy, speed, and cost. Industry-authored (Onit legal-spend-management vendor).

## Tasks covered (task design)
- 50 legal invoices (anonymised client invoices + human-validated synthetic examples), 492 line items across expense categories and formats.
- Violation types embedded: late submission, block billing (multiple unrelated tasks in one entry without itemisation), unauthorised tasks, etc.
- Two levels: invoice-level approve/dispute decision; line-item compliance classification.
- Models: o1, GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Claude 3.7 Sonnet (extended thinking), Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking Experimental 01-21, DeepSeek R1 (self-hosted). Non-agentic single-LLM evaluation (no tool loop).

## Eval method
Ground truth set by a panel of nine experienced invoice reviewers (legal bill review specialists, current/former legal professionals). Human baselines grouped by post-qualification experience. Metrics: F-score on approve/dispute decisions and line-item classification, seconds per invoice, cost per invoice.

## Key numbers (quotes)
- "LLMs achieve up to 92% accuracy, surpassing the 72% ceiling set by experienced lawyers" (invoice approval decisions).
- "LLMs dominate line-item classification, with top models reaching F-scores of 81%, compared to just 43% for the best-performing human group."
- Invoice-level F-scores: Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking 0.9200, GPT-4o 0.9200; Claude 3.7 Sonnet / Claude 3.5 Sonnet / o1 0.840; DeepSeek R1 0.820.
- Line-item F-scores: Gemini 2.0 Flash Thinking 0.8058; Claude 3.7 Sonnet 0.6426; GPT-4o 0.6307; Claude 3.5 Sonnet 0.5218.
- "while lawyers take 194 to 316 seconds per invoice, LLMs are capable of completing reviews in as fast as 3.6 seconds" (GPT-4o 3.61 s).
- "AI slashes review expenses by 99.97%, reducing invoice processing costs from an average of $4.27 per invoice for human invoice reviewers to mere cents."
- Noted caveat: about "20% of human invoice reviewers' decisions exhibit subjectivity" (willingness to deviate from strict guideline adherence).
