## Abstract (condensed, from fetched text)
Reframes LLM-based trading agents as expert-system decision pipelines and presents an audit-oriented evidence map of 77 included studies (protocol-coded snapshot screened through 2026-03-09). Primary empirical subset n=19 (Action Output + Closed-Loop Evaluation); remaining 58 retained as background. Central finding: protocol incomparability — only 2/19 report time-consistent split protocols, 1/19 explicit transaction-cost model, 1/19 universe/survivorship handling, 11/19 execution timing/semantics, 15/19 coded R0, none reach R3 reproducibility. Uses Architecture-Capability-Adaptation as working lens; contributions are the evidence ledger, reproducibility audit, and reporting checklist.
