# A Scoping Review of ChatGPT Research in Accounting and Finance

- **ID:** arXiv:2412.05731 (dated July 2024 in-document) — verified from https://arxiv.org/html/2412.05731v1 (full text)
- **Authors:** Mengming Michael Dong (Missouri State), Theophanis C. Stratopoulos (Waterloo), Victor Xiaoqi Wang (CSU Long Beach)
- **Status:** INCLUDE — category: survey-adjacent (related-work survey; NOT a system/benchmark; no leaf cells scored)

## What it is
Scoping review of 68 published + SSRN working papers (Jan 2022 – Mar 2024) on ChatGPT/LLMs in accounting and finance, organized by an input–process–output adoption framework. "In a relatively short period (from January 2022 to March 2024), 264 papers related to ChatGPT and other LLMs were uploaded to SSRN within the Accounting, Finance, or Economics networks."

## FUNCTION topics covered (the question we screened for)
Organizing frame = AAA/AFA research areas (Table 4), NOT CFO-office functions:
- Accounting: **Accounting Information Systems, Auditing, Education, Financial Accounting and Reporting, Management Accounting, Taxation**
- Finance: **Asset Pricing and Investment, Corporate Finance, Education, Risk Management**

Function-relevant content:
- **Audit** (Table 9): 3 clusters — audit efficiency/automation (Emett et al.; Eulerich & Wood), frameworks for risk-based audit integration, auditor-vs-AI cognition.
- **Financial reporting** (Table 10): 4 clusters — ESG/sustainability reporting, reporting quality/transparency, report enrichment, corporate responses to GenAI. Mostly disclosure ANALYSIS (investor side), not statement preparation; future-work call: "investigate the potential of LLMs to streamline transaction recording and financial report generation ... such as classifying transactions or generating draft financial reports."
- **Tax** (Table 11): practitioner tax research QA (Alarie et al. Blue J vs ChatGPT — vendor COI noted by reviewers themselves); GPT-4-derived tax-audit disclosure measure (Choi & Kim). "the application of LLMs in this field has been limited."
- **AIS** (Table 12): LLM+RPA integration proposals (Beerbaum; Li & Vasarhelyi), O'Leary chatbot comparisons.
- Finance side is markets/investor-facing (sentiment, portfolio, advisory, central-bank text) — outside our tree.

## Relevance to our survey (differentiation)
- Pre-agentic: reviews ChatGPT-era single-LLM studies and adoption implications; no agent architectures, no benchmarks-with-metrics inventory, no task-level coverage matrix.
- Organized by academic research area (AAA/AFA), not by finance-FUNCTION task tree; closest overlap is that its Audit/Reporting/Tax/AIS sections name the same territory our taxonomy formalizes.
- Use as: related-work citation for "non-agentic AI-in-accounting reviews" band (novelty_check.md already anticipated this class); also a source of adoption-motivation citations (Gartner >80% enterprise adoption prediction by 2026, cited to Cooney 2023).

## Eval method / data realism / agent scope
Not applicable (literature review; no system, no benchmark). Method: 4-step scoping protocol (framework, search w/ eligibility criteria, quality assessment, synthesis); 68 papers classified (Potential applications 64.7%, Conceptual 19.1%, Value realization 16.2%).
