# CoffeeBench: Benchmarking Long-Horizon LLM Agents in Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Economies

- **ID:** arXiv:2606.16613 (v1, cs.AI, 15 Jun 2026) — verified from https://arxiv.org/html/2606.16613v1 (full text)
- **Authors:** Issa Sugiura, Taro Makino, Takashi Ishida (Sakana AI); Daichi Hattori, Kazuo Araragi, Keita Ogawa, Shota Onose, Teppei Usuki (KPMG AZSA LLC)
- **Code/data:** github.com/SakanaAI/CoffeeBench; trajectories at pub.sakana.ai/coffeebench
- **Status:** INCLUDE — category: benchmark (business-economy simulation; working-capital mechanics partial)

## Summary
Long-horizon multi-agent economy benchmark: six LLM agent firms (2 coffee farmers, 2 roasters, 2 retailers) autonomously operate over a 90-day simulated coffee supply chain, "each seeking to maximize cumulative net income through communication and transactions while managing cash, inventory, and pricing." Evaluated model plays roaster_A; other five firms are fixed reference agents (Claude Sonnet 4.6). Hundreds to thousands of tool calls per run. Positions itself against Vending-Bench (single firm) and Vending-Bench Arena (homogeneous firms): CoffeeBench has 3 heterogeneous economic roles.

## Finance-function content (the AR/AP/cash question)
- Each agent's operational state includes "cash balance, inventory, accounts receivable, and accounts payable" (Sec 3.2).
- Sec 3.3: "Upon delivery, an invoice is issued under a default net-30 payment term (payment due within 30 days). Late payments incur interest"; shared tool `pay_invoice()` "settle outstanding payables"; offers specify "price, quantity, and payment terms".
- Sec 3.5: negative cash balance → bankruptcy; "unpaid obligations are written off as bad debt for creditors"; constraints require agents "to balance cash flow, inventory risk, pricing, and counterparty reliability".
- KPI (Sec 3.6): NetIncome = Revenue − COGS − OpEx − InterestExp + InterestRev; OpEx includes bad-debt expense + inventory writedowns; interest terms = accrued late fees on overdue payables/receivables. 0.1%/day late-payment penalty; all firms start with $15,000 cash.
- BUT the agent is a whole-firm operator (production, pricing, negotiation, trading) — not a finance-office role; there is no invoice-processing/matching, no collections workflow, no treasury desk. Working capital is an embedded sim mechanic, not the evaluated task.

## Leaf cells (justified)
- **3.1:P** cash/liquidity management — cash-flow balancing under net-30 credit, late-payment interest, bankruptcy-on-negative-cash is a core survival constraint; partial because it is folded into general business management, with no forecasting/positioning task or treasury tooling.
- **7.1:P** P2P — agent procures inputs via offers with payment terms and settles payables with `pay_invoice()`; partial because invoices are sim-generated (no capture/matching/approval workflow).
- **7.2:P** O2C — agent sells on credit (chooses/accepts payment terms; receivables accrue interest; bad-debt exposure to counterparties); partial because invoicing is automatic and there is no billing/collections/dunning task.

## Eval method
Simulation KPI: cumulative net income over 90 days (mean±std over 3 runs); behavioral telemetry (tool calls, idle days, DMs sent, API cost); baselines = PassiveRoaster (no actions) and HeuristicRoaster; exploratory revenue-maximizing stress test + approximate performance-ceiling (headroom) analysis. No LLM judge, no human raters.

## Data realism
Fully synthetic simulated economy (stochastic logistics, spoilage, parametric consumer-demand model with brand loyalty). Limitations section flags "Gap between simulation and real-world markets" and statistical reliability (3 runs).

## Agent scope
ReAct single agent under evaluation inside a 6-agent heterogeneous economy; unrestricted P2P trade + free-form messaging (reselling and multi-hop trades possible). Background agents fixed (Sonnet 4.6).

## Key numbers (Table 2, net income $, mean±std of 3 runs)
- GPT-5.5: +3,109±1,123 (best); Claude Opus 4.7: +2,782±2,263; Claude Sonnet 4.6: +2,236±1,489; Gemini 3.1 Pro: +1,695±508; GLM-5.1: +1,597±1,199; Kimi K2.6: +454±1,420; Claude Haiku 4.5: −630±1,745 (idle-drift: 40±22 idle days); HeuristicRoaster −1,931±429; PassiveRoaster −2,765.
- Headline: "all models outperform a passive baseline that takes no actions, with most achieving positive net income"; "Claude Haiku 4.5 exhibits an *idle-drift* failure mode, repeatedly choosing inaction despite producing coherent assessments and plans."
