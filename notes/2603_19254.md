# From Comprehension to Reasoning: A Hierarchical Benchmark for Automated Financial Research Reporting (FinReasoning)

- **ID:** arXiv:2603.19254 (v1) — verified from https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19254v1 (full text)
- **Authors:** Yiyun Zhu, Yidong Jiang, Ziwen Xu, Yinsheng Yao, Dawei Cheng, Jinru Ding, Yejie Zheng, Jie Xu (Tongji University; Shanghai AI Laboratory; East Money Information)
- **Status:** INCLUDE — category: benchmark-adjacent (analyst research-report generation; ADJ band, not CFO-office function)

## Summary
FinReasoning is a Chinese-language benchmark decomposing financial (equity/industry) research-report generation into three tracks aligned with sell-side analyst workflows: Semantic Consistency (error localization/explanation/correction in long financial text), Data Alignment (numerical verification, indicator computation, rule-based reasoning over A-share market data), and Deep Insight (research-grade analysis scored by a 4-dimension/12-indicator expert rubric). Evaluates 19 LLMs; finds an "understanding-execution gap" and weak performance of finance fine-tuned models. Resource: https://github.com/TongjiFinLab/FinReasoning.

## Tasks
- 4,800 evaluation items total (Sec 3.1): SC = 1,800, DA = 1,800, DI = 1,200 (Table 1).
- 3 tracks, each with multi-level sub-tasks (SC: terminology/fact/logic errors; DA: verification, calculation, reasoning; DI: rubric-scored analysis).
- 19 LLMs evaluated (closed, open, finance fine-tuned).

## Eval method
Hybrid rubric + LLM-judge: sentence-level Precision/Recall/F1 for error localization; BERTScore/SimCSE for semantic alignment; "LLM-as-a-Judge mechanism that assigns fine-grained scores... along four dimensions: correctness, reasoning rigor, relevance, and completeness" (Sec 3.3); DA uses success rate + answer accuracy with rule-computed ground truth ("executed through a unified calculator", Sec 3.2.2); DI uses expert rubric via LLM-judge (DeepSeek-V3 as judge, Table 2). Judge validation: "BERTScore and SimCSE achieve Pearson correlations of 0.86 and 0.91 with LLM-Judge" (Sec 4.2.1).

## Data realism
Mixed: real source materials ("677 pieces of financial literature, over 10,000 publicly available news reports, and 901 Chinese financial research reports" from Eastmoney Choice; real A-share market data 2023-2025, Sec 3.1), but SC long-form texts are LLM-generated with injected errors and QAs are generated by DeepSeek-V3 (Sec 3.2) — i.e., real-grounded but synthetic/templated task instances.

## Agent scope
QA / long-form generation evaluation of single models; no tool-use environment, no multi-agent system (DA answers computed by benchmark-side calculator, not agent execution).

## Key numbers (quoted)
- Abstract: "no model achieves overwhelming superiority across all three tracks; Doubao-Seed-1.8, GPT-5, and Kimi-K2 rank as the top three in overall performance".
- Sec 4.2.1 Finding 1: "Error Explanation scores are consistently higher than Correct Content scores, with gaps of 20.2 points for Terminology, 9.7 for Fact, and 24.3 for Logic."
- Sec 4.2.2 Finding 1: "average performance degrades monotonically from Ver. (79.8) to Cal. (57.6) to Reason. (50.9), with a mean decay rate of 36.6%."
- Table 5 (DA Reason.): GPT-5 Success Rate 100.0, Answer Acc. 63.0, Overall 68.5; DianJin-R1-7B falls to 6.9 overall ("an 89.5% decline, driven largely by its success rate dropping to 16.0%", Sec 4.2.2).

## Leaf tags (justified)
- **ADJ ●** — the entire benchmark "mirrors the workflow of research analysts" (Sec 3.2.3): equity/industry research-report generation for investment use, i.e., analyst-research band. No CFO-office leaf (close, FP&A, treasury, tax, IR, P2P/O2C, statutory reporting) is exercised; external research reports are not management reporting (2.4) nor variance analysis (2.3).
