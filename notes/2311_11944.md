## Summary
Open-book financial QA test suite over public-company filings: 10,231 question–answer–evidence triplets covering 40 US-listed companies and 361 public filings (2015–2023: 10-Ks 75% of documents, plus 10-Qs, 8-Ks, earnings reports). Three question types: 25 reusable "domain-relevant" questions (925 entries), 1,323 annotator-written "novel generated" questions, and 7,983 template-based "metrics-generated" questions built from 18 base metrics extracted from the three primary statements. 16 model configurations (GPT-4/GPT-4-Turbo, Claude2, Llama2 across closed-book / oracle / single & shared vector store / long-context, two prompt orders) evaluated on a 150-case sample with all 2,400 answers manually labeled. Designed as a "minimum performance standard"; even so, realistic retrieval configs fail badly.

## Tasks covered
Investor-side filings QA (single company, single turn): information extraction (28%), numerical reasoning (66%), logical reasoning (6%) per the paper's own taxonomy (n=8,908 typed questions). Examples: "What is Boeing's FY2022 cost of goods sold (in USD millions)?"; domain-relevant questions like dividend payment or operating-margin consistency; metrics-generated questions compute base/derivative metrics (e.g., net income margin) from income statement / balance sheet / cash-flow statement, sometimes across statements. Explicitly no cross-company comparison, no multi-turn.

## Eval method
Manual expert review: each of 16 configurations' responses to the 150-case sample labeled by the research team as Correct / Incorrect / Failure-to-answer (n=2,400), with different-but-valid answers credited; qualitative theme analysis (high-quality answers, hallucinations, helpful refusals, irrelevant comments). No automated metric, no LLM judge.

## Data realism
Real public filings (static PDFs from IR pages/EDGAR); 20 finance annotators recruited (treasury analysts, finance MBAs, junior analysts; 5 dropped for quality), senior-analyst review of 10–20% weekly, ~10% end-of-project re-review. Dataset fields include evidence string + 1-indexed page number + full page text. Authors flag: some questions "ecologically valid but simplistic"; some gold answers contestable.

## Agent scope
Not agentic — single-turn QA under closed-book, oracle, RAG (Chroma/Langchain, text-embedding-ada-002; single- vs shared-store), and long-context (95–100k-token truncation) configurations. No tool use ("adding additional 'tools' such as calculators and APIs, could drive better performance" is future work, §7). Tested November 2023; temperature 0.01.
